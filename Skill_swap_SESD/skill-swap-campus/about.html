About the project

Learning is better
when it is shared.

Skill Swap Campus is a peer-learning platform concept built to help students exchange knowledge, build confidence and connect across campus.

The opportunity

Talent is everywhere.
Access should be too.

Students learn useful things in class, at work and from each other. Yet it can be hard to find someone nearby who can help with a particular skill. Paid lessons are not accessible to everyone, and informal knowledge-sharing can be difficult to organize.

This academic prototype makes peer learning easier to discover and coordinate—with an exchange of skills rather than money.

A campus-wide circle of learning

One student’s strength can become another student’s breakthrough. Everyone can be both a learner and a teacher.

What we are building

From a learning gap to a shared solution

Problem

Students often cannot easily find affordable, approachable peer support for practical skills outside formal courses.

Objectives

Make skills discoverable, enable fair exchanges and create a supportive cross-department learning community.

Solution

A simple student directory for offering skills, finding a match, sending requests and tracking exchanges.

Why peer learning?

Every exchange helps both sides grow.

Teaching reinforces understanding. Learning from a peer makes it easier to ask questions, try things out and learn at your own pace.

Stronger understanding

Explaining an idea is a powerful way to deepen your own knowledge.

Real campus connections

Build relationships with curious students and diverse perspectives.

Room to grow

Practice practical skills in a low-pressure, collaborative setting.

Looking ahead

Designed to grow with the community.

Future enhancements could include verified college accounts, real-time messaging, calendar scheduling, feedback and ratings, accessibility improvements and a secure hosted backend.

This frontend-only build is a learning prototype, not an official campus service.
